Mother Losing Soul

Where is our world heading? What will it become? Does the future lack soul? How is it now—do we lack soul?

Concept & Inspiration

Religion is very inspiring to me. I think humans will always have faith, but in what changes a lot. It is not long that I am religious now and looking at other people and seeing how everyones family is so understanding of it, as if they truly have a belief was very surprising for me. I thought not many people believed in God. I have my ideas about future, I wish to think optimistically, but the way we are heading now is ripping the soul from our creations. So my idea was to bring something in the future world that it lacks. The mother is cracked, the soul slowly disappears.

Research & Exploration

I have researched different styles of icons, what each one means, what symbolism it holds. I was drawing, sketching, making notes, defining the concept.

Sketches & Prototypes

I made a small scale prototype of an icon first, then it got broken because of not firing properly. But I already had an idea in my mind. Then I started to move that to a big clay sculpture. I had to understand what way would I be able to build it. The method of wrapping clay around wood covered with fabric and materials worked well. The clay dried out nicely. When it was leather hard, I removed the mold and it dried on its own. Before that, I had lots of variations with making shapes.

Development & Iteration

I used flowers, because they represent the spirit of the unconscious world. I used roses, lilies and passion flower—most symbolic flowers. Sculpting was the easiest and most enjoyable process for me. I liked touching and feeling the face, trying the image come to mind and not me make the image. I wanted it to go through my body, as if I would act as a transmitter. Glazing took a lot of time, lots of experiments, month of working and researching which one to use. I made the decision after my experiments, but on the final piece colors came out different. I think the piece has its own likes and dislikes because it became very personalized. When looking at it from afar you think you know what you look at, but the details speak about this piece. If they find it thousands years after, scientists will be the ones examining it, that would know: why blue, why this hole, why the engraving on the back side of the icon? After 2 glazings and a crack which I am glad about—that was my first thought and intention at the beginning, but I couldn't do it because of the lack of skill. The piece did it for me and I was happy. In the end I didn't like one glaze part on Jesus so I fixed it up with nail polish. Did some color mixing before.

Reflection & Learning

Future traces

My grief, hurt and sorrow that I have about this world I wanted to express through my piece. I feel whatever you feel when you look at the expressions I molded. I hate to see people being one sided. I love technologies but forgetting who we are and what other potential we have is falling into Ahriman's trap.

I think at least the feeling in what you make should be understandable for everyone, even a random person from the street, so I tried to be clear with my intentions of transferring emotions. What I could not do in physical life I should by using 3D modeling and created an animation. That's how I imagined my icon shining sunshine rays as it stands (and yes it stands but it's very risky and not stable). I used engraving that I did with 3D printing and the words said:

Helia that's me
Do not forget the spirit ours

It is engraved in Georgian, so the explorers would have a better understanding of who was responsible for the piece.

In conclusion, I tried to express what troubles me now about future, how I imagine it to be and create a work that embodies the qualities of a future life. Broken Mary—broken spirit. Mary being painted only, she is in center, she is blue, melancholy.
